WEBSITE PRIVACY

Privacy on otlfrosta.com

Last updated: October 26, 2026.

Optional website analytics

This personal website uses Google Analytics 4 (GA4) only if you select "Allow analytics". Before you consent, no Google Analytics script is loaded and this site does not send analytics events to Google. If you consent, GA4 may use cookies and collect standard website measurements such as page views, approximate location, referral source, device and browser information, and clicks to the Chrome Web Store. Google processes this information under its own privacy terms.

We use these measurements to understand which pages visitors find useful and which links lead to the Chrome Web Store. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, conversation text, or other personal content as analytics event parameters.

Your choice

Your analytics choice is kept in your browser's local storage. Choose "No thanks" to decline, or select "Analytics settings" in the footer to change your choice at any time. Revoking consent stops subsequent website analytics loading and clears Google Analytics cookies for this site where accessible. Browser privacy tools may block analytics independently.

Chrome extension

Website analytics are separate from the Gemini Exporter extension. The extension has no developer telemetry or conversation uploads, and export processing remains local. Its separate extension privacy policy explains those protections.
