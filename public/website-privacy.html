About this website. We use Cloudflare to serve and protect otlfrosta.com and its cookie-free Web Analytics to understand site usage. The Gemini Exporter extension is separate and has its own Extension Privacy Policy.

1. What this policy covers

This policy applies to visits to otlfrosta.com, including its pages and outbound links. It does not apply to the Gemini Exporter Chrome extension, which processes exported conversations locally without developer-operated analytics or upload servers. Please read the Extension Privacy Policy for the extension's data practices.

2. Cookie-free Cloudflare Web Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics for aggregated information about visits and site performance, such as page views, referring websites, approximate country, browser or device categories, and page load performance. Web Analytics does not use analytics cookies, Local Storage, or browser fingerprinting.

Under our current Cloudflare EU Lite configuration, European Union visitor metrics are excluded from Web Analytics. We do not use Google Analytics (GA4) or an advertising tracker on this website. We do not collect or analyze the contents of your AI conversations through this website.

3. Hosting, security, and technical requests

Cloudflare delivers and protects this website. In doing so, Cloudflare may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, request timestamps, and security-related data, as necessary to provide its infrastructure services. This is separate from our cookie-free Web Analytics reports. For more information, see Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

We do not operate a website login or a developer-hosted database containing your exported conversations. We do not sell website visitors' personal information.

4. Outbound links and campaign labels

Links to the Chrome Web Store may include URL parameters such as utm_source, utm_medium, and utm_campaign to identify which campaign referred the visit. Our website may pass these labels along when you choose to open the store listing. They are campaign labels, not a visitor ID, and our website does not use cookies to associate your visits across services.

When you leave our site for Chrome Web Store, GitHub, or another service, that service's privacy policy and analytics practices apply.

5. Questions and updates

For questions about this policy, contact us through GitHub Issues. Please do not include private conversations or login credentials in public issues. We may update this policy when the website's services or practices change, and will update the date shown above.

简体中文

关于本网站。我们使用 Cloudflare 托管和保护 otlfrosta.com，并通过不依赖 Cookie 的 Web Analytics 了解网站访问情况。Gemini Exporter 扩展有独立的扩展隐私政策。

1. 政策适用范围

本政策适用于访问 otlfrosta.com 官网及其页面、外部链接的行为，不适用于 Gemini Exporter Chrome 扩展。扩展的对话导出在本地完成，不使用开发者提供的分析或上传服务器。详情请参阅扩展隐私政策。

2. 不使用分析 Cookie 的 Cloudflare Web Analytics

我们使用 Cloudflare Web Analytics 获取汇总的访问与性能数据，包括页面浏览量、来源网站、大致国家、浏览器及设备类别和页面加载性能。Web Analytics 不使用分析 Cookie、Local Storage 或浏览器指纹。

根据目前启用的 Cloudflare EU Lite 设置，欧盟访客的 Web Analytics 数据被排除。官网不使用 Google Analytics（GA4）或广告追踪器，也不会通过官网分析或收集您的 AI 对话内容。

3. 网站托管、安全与网络请求

Cloudflare 负责本网站的内容分发与安全防护，可能为提供基础设施服务而处理 IP 地址、请求时间、安全相关数据等必要的技术信息。这不同于不使用 Cookie 的 Web Analytics 报表。详情请参阅 Cloudflare 隐私政策。

官网不提供登录账号，也不运营储存您导出对话的数据库。我们不出售官网访客的个人信息。

4. 外部链接与推广活动标签

Chrome Web Store 链接可能携带 utm_source、utm_medium、utm_campaign 等网址参数，用于标识访问来自哪项推广活动。当您主动打开商店页面时，官网可能向商店透传这些活动标签。它们不包含本站生成的访客 ID，官网也不使用 Cookie 将您在不同网站上的访问行为关联起来。

离开官网前往 Chrome Web Store、GitHub 或其他服务后，相关服务独立的隐私政策和分析方式将适用。

5. 联系与政策更新

如对本政策有疑问，可通过 GitHub Issues 联系。请勿在公开 Issue 中包含私人对话或登录凭据。如网站所用服务或数据处理方式发生变化，我们可能更新本政策，并修改页面顶部日期。
